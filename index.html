Comparative Analysis of the Effectiveness of Sampling Approaches for Decision Tree Classifier

Afolabi Doyin, Victor Daudu, Sennaike Oladipupo, Adetunji P. Adewole The Journal of Computer Science and Its Applications · Vol. 30, No. 2, December 2023

When the class you care about most is rare, standard classifiers tend to miss it. This study compares common resampling techniques with the sampling approach built into the Minority Entropy Decision Tree (MEDT) to see which best reduces misclassification of the minority class.

0.958 sensitivity on the cerebral stroke dataset with MEDT
0.53 sensitivity on the thoracic dataset — best of all methods tested
methods compared
Random under-sampling (RUS) Random over-sampling (ROS) SMOTE MEDT (ID3 variant)
abstract, in brief

Imbalanced datasets show up across many real-world problems: one class makes up only a small share of the data, yet getting it wrong is costly. Sampling is one way to tackle this. The study applied random under-sampling, random over-sampling and SMOTE, alongside the sampling approach embedded in MEDT — a variant of the ID3 decision tree — to imbalanced datasets before classifying them with decision trees.

MEDT's approach outperformed the others, and the paper recommends it for handling minority-class misclassification in imbalanced classification.

Keywords: imbalanced dataset · decision trees · sampling · classification
